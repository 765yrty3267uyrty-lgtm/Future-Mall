Usage Guidelines

How to use these posters effectively

🏢

Workplace

Print A3 size for break rooms, hallways, and near workstations. Rotate monthly for visibility.

🏫

Classroom

Use A4 size for student handouts. Incorporate into digital citizenship curriculum.

🏠

Home

Print A4 for family fridge or home office. Discuss one poster per week at family meetings.

💻

Digital Display

Use PNG versions for digital signage, intranet, Slack/Teams channels, or email signatures.

Spread Cybersecurity Awareness

Download, print, and share these posters. Every person who learns to spot a phishing email or create a strong password makes the digital world safer for everyone.
