Remember These Red Flags

Quick reference for spotting phishing

🚨

Urgent Language

"Immediate action required", "Account will be closed", "Urgent: verify now"

👤

Generic Greetings

"Dear Customer", "Hello User", "Dear Sir/Madam" instead of your name

🔗

Suspicious Links

Hover to check real URL. Mismatched domains, URL shorteners, slight misspellings

✍️

Poor Grammar/Spelling

Excessive caps, spelling errors, mismatched fonts, unprofessional formatting

📎

Unexpected Attachments

.exe, .zip, .scr, .pdf with macros, unexpected invoices or receipts

💳

Requests for Personal Info

Legitimate companies never ask for passwords, SSN, or credit cards via email
