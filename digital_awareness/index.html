Essential Cybersecurity Tips

Quick reference for daily digital safety

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use 12+ characters with mixed case, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords across sites.

🔐

Enable 2FA/MFA

Enable multi-factor authentication on all important accounts. Use authenticator apps, not SMS.

🔗

Verify URLs

Hover over links before clicking. Check for HTTPS and correct domain spelling.

📧

Email Caution

Don't open attachments from unknown senders. Verify urgent requests through another channel.

🔄

Keep Updated

Enable automatic updates for OS, browser, and apps. Security patches protect against exploits.

💾

Backup Data

Follow 3-2-1 rule: 3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite. Test restores regularly.
