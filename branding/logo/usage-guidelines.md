# Logo Usage Guidelines

How to apply the Future Mall logo correctly across all touchpoints.

## Available Variations

Located in [`final/`](final/):

| File | Use Case |
|------|----------|
| `future-mall-logo-primary.svg` | Default horizontal lockup (color, light bg) |
| `future-mall-logo-stacked.svg` | Square/avatar/social contexts |
| `future-mall-icon.svg` | Favicons, app icons, small UI footprints |
| `future-mall-logo-black.svg` | Single-color black on light backgrounds |
| `future-mall-logo-white.svg` | Single-color white on dark backgrounds |

## Clear Space

Always keep a margin around the logo at least **equal to the height of the
wordmark's 'F'**. Nothing should intrude into this space — no text, no graphics.

## Minimum Sizes

- Horizontal / stacked logo: **80px** wide minimum on screen.
- Icon only: **32px** minimum on screen.
- Print: the logo must remain legible at the intended physical size; prefer
  SVG/vector for print.

## Backgrounds

| Background | Correct Variation |
|------------|-------------------|
| Light / white | Primary, or Black |
| Brand dark (`#1E293B`) | White |
| Photographic | White (with subtle scrim if needed) |

## What Not To Do

- Do not stretch, squash, or rotate the logo.
- Do not apply drop shadows, glows, gradients, or outlines.
- Do not change the brand colors of the logo.
- Do not rearrange or recreate lockup elements.
- Do not place the logo on high-contrast busy patterns.
- Do not add extra words/subtitles inside the logo.
