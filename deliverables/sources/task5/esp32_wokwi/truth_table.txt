Smart Garage Gate - Truth Table
================================
Project: Future Mall - Task 5 (ESP32 + MicroPython, Wokwi)

Legend:
  C   = car count (0..15)
  IN  = IN button pressed   (1 = pressed)
  OUT = OUT button pressed  (1 = pressed)
  green  LED = free space available (C < 15)
  red    LED = garage FULL (C = 15)
  yellow LED = a car is ENTERING (blinks on IN)
  blue   LED = a car is EXITING  (blinks on OUT)

--- Free-space / status LEDs (steady state) -------------------------------

  C        | free?  | green | red   | meaning
  ---------+--------+-------+-------+------------------------
  0        | yes    | ON    | OFF   | empty garage
  1 .. 14  | yes    | ON    | OFF   | parking available
  15       | no     | OFF   | ON    | FULL - reject entry

--- Buttons (one-shot actions) -------------------------------------------

  IN  | OUT | action                    | count | yellow | blue
  ----+-----+---------------------------+-------+--------+-----
  0   | 0   | none                      | same  | OFF    | OFF
  1   | 0   | car ENTERS (if C < 15)    | +1    | flash  | OFF
  1   | 0   | car ENTERS (if C = 15)    | same  | OFF    | OFF  (FULL -> reject, red flash)
  0   | 1   | car EXITS (if C > 0)      | -1    | OFF    | flash
  0   | 1   | car EXITS (if C = 0)      | same  | OFF    | OFF  (empty -> nothing to exit)
  1   | 1   | both pressed              | same  | n/a    | n/a  (debounced, one action max)

--- Complete example run (starting at C = 0) -------------------------------
  Step | event  | C (before->after) | green | red | yellow | blue
  -----+--------+-------------------+-------+-----+--------+-----
  1    | IN     | 0 -> 1            | ON    | OFF | flash  | OFF
  2    | IN     | 1 -> 2            | ON    | OFF | flash  | OFF
  ...  | IN x13 | 2 -> 15           | OFF   | ON  | flash  | OFF
  3    | IN     | 15 -> 15 (FULL)   | OFF   | ON  | OFF    | OFF   (rejected)
  4    | OUT    | 15 -> 14          | ON    | OFF | OFF    | flash
  5    | OUT    | 1 -> 0            | ON    | OFF | OFF    | flash
  6    | OUT    | 0 -> 0 (EMPTY)    | ON    | OFF | OFF    | OFF   (nothing to exit)

Note: flows/truth follow a Moore-style state machine - the LED state is a pure
function of the current counter value (plus transient blink pulses for entry/exit).
